case in it is a test in `hr_suite/hr_suite/test_sft_uat_fixes.py` (34 tests, all passing on the
scratch site `hrsuite-dev.localhost`, KSA and Bahrain). Run them with:

```
bench --site SITE run-tests --app hr_suite --module hr_suite.hr_suite.test_sft_uat_fixes
```

features that this PR adds, so they are fixed *by deploying it*; the rest needed new work, below.

## Behaviour to know about

- **The monthly job credits at month end.** On 12 June the balance is 29.75; the 0.70 for June appears
  in *Accruing This Month* and is credited on 30 June. The consultant's "30.45" is the sum of both.
- **The three switches are still OFF on existing sites** (Leave Accrual Engine, Split Sick Leave, Expense
  Claim routing). Bugs 3, 5, 8, 9 and 10 only show on a country whose Leave Accrual Engine is *HR Suite
  Ledger*; switch Saudi Arabia (and Bahrain) on after importing opening balances.
- **New Country Config fields** (all with defaults that change nothing until chosen):
  *Pay Accrued Leave On Exit Below Minimum Service* (on), *Annual Leave Before Minimum Service* (Allow),
  *Sick Leave Pay Window* (Leave Period). *Minimum Earned Leave-Salary Days* is now 0.
- **Payroll adjustments were proved with the payroll calls mocked**, not against a real submitted Salary
  Slip. Run K11 in the UAT guide on `sft-uat` once deployed.
- Values the spec marks **verify** (Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain SIO) are unchanged and still need the
  client's adviser.

## Round 3: Leave Salary Recovery payroll defect (G1, 24 Oct 2026)

Spec: *SFT-UAT Leave Salary Settlement, Developer Functional Specification*. Observed on `sft-uat`: employee G1, Annual Leave 1-21 Oct (18 leave days), settlement LSS-2026-0002, last working day 30 Sep, monthly gross 700. Two Leave Salary Recovery deductions were booked, 700 on 30 Sep (HR-ADS-26-10-00003) and 490 on 1 Oct (HR-ADS-26-10-00004), and the draft September slip `Sal Slip/G1/00002` came out at gross 700, net 0.

| Question in the spec | Root cause (read from the live records and the code) | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Why 700 on 30 Sep, against a completed September? | `Pay Worked Days In Settlement` is on for SA. The settlement pays "the days worked this month", 1st of the month to the last working day. With the last working day 30 Sep that is all of September (30 days, 700, `worked_days_pay` on the document). To avoid paying it twice, `_desired_exclusions` then booked a 700 deduction on the September payslip. Money-neutral on paper (700 paid by the settlement instead of the slip) but it zeroes a month that was fully worked and could simply be paid by payroll. | The settlement pays worked days only while the month of the last working day is still open, that is when the leave starts in that same month. When the leave starts in a later month, `pay_worked_days_in_settlement` is set to 0 on the document, no worked-days pay line is made and nothing is taken off the earlier month's payslip. Mid-month departures behave exactly as before. |
| Why 490 on 1 Oct, and is it right? | 490 = 21 calendar days (1-21 Oct) x 700/30. The October exclusion was spread over the *calendar* days of the leave, while the settlement pays *leave days* (`total_leave_days` = 18, which excluded three Fridays at the time; 18 x 23.333 = 420). So payroll kept out 70 more than the settlement paid. An exclusion is required by the design (HRMS pays an approved annual-leave day in the payslip, the settlement pays it too); the amount was wrong. | The exclusion is now `leave days x monthly gross / 30`, month by month, counted as the Leave Application counts them (`get_number_of_leave_days`, so the leave type's holiday setting decides). Across a whole leave the months add up to the application's own total, a half day included. For G1 this gives 420, the same days the settlement pays. |
| Leave and settlement day counts differ | `Annual Leave` `include_holiday` was changed 0 -> 1 on 23 Oct 2026 (after the leave and the settlement were made). HRMS now counts the same dates as 21 leave days; the stored application still says 18. | Not a code fault. New applications count 21. The exclusion follows the days stored on the settlement, so it stays equal to what was paid. |
| Currency | The settlement and both Additional Salary rows are in BHD (company Steel Force Trading WLL, Salary Structure currency). `Country Config SA` says SAR, which is the country's reference currency and is not used for these amounts. | None. Amounts are in the employee's payroll currency throughout. |
| Duplicates on retry / overlap | Re-running the exclusion sync books nothing when the rows already match; a second settlement for a leave whose days are all paid is refused ("Nothing To Settle"); a second open draft for the same leave is refused. | Pinned by tests, no code change. |

Kept as they were (spec section 9): `Leave Salary Recovery` stays a Deduction and its backend validation is untouched; the Salary Structure Assignment is never touched; sick leave and other leave types never reach this code; rejoin handling (early, late, extension) is unchanged.

**One design point for a decision.** The spec (6.2) asks that already-settled leave days be kept out of normal earnings *without* using a recovery deduction as the substitute. The product's mechanism has always been an Additional Salary deduction on the covering payslip (the same one the older Annual Leave Disbursement uses). This fix keeps that mechanism and corrects what it deducts and when. Replacing it with a payment-days adjustment inside the Salary Slip calculation is a larger payroll change and was not made.

**Tests** (`test_leave_salary_settlement.py`, class `TestCompletedMonthAndLeaveDays`, all failing on the old code and passing now): TC01 completed month stays in payroll; same-month leave still pays worked days; exclusion in leave days (18) not calendar days (21); holidays counted as leave (21); two-month leave split in leave days; TC06 second settlement of a fully paid leave and two open drafts refused; TC13 repeated sync books nothing new.

**Existing records on `sft-uat` (not changed).** LSS-2026-0002 is submitted, so it was not edited. Safe correction: cancel its Payment Advice if one is live, cancel the settlement (this cancels HR-ADS-26-10-00003 and -00004, the September slip is still a draft), re-create it from HR-LAP-2026-00011 with last working day 30 Sep. After the fix it pays 420 (no worked days), and books a single 420 deduction on 1 Oct. Then re-run the September slip: gross 700, no recovery, net 700.
