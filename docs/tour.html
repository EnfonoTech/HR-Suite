Leave Allocation

HR Suite › Leave · Grants leave balance to an employee for a period
Leave
Path: HR Suite → Leave → Leave Allocation → New

Create one allocation per leave type per employee per year. Annual leave must be allocated before employees can apply.

1
Required · Link
Employee Link → Employee
Select the employee receiving the allocation.
Ahmed EMP-0042 Fatima EMP-0089 James EMP-0156
2
Required · Link
Leave Type Link → Leave Type
Choose from Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Maternity Leave, etc.
Ahmed Annual Leave Fatima Sick Leave James Annual Leave
💡 KSA Article 109: minimum 21 days/year for <5 yrs service, 30 days/year after 5 yrs.
3
Required · Date
From Date / To Date Date
The validity period of this allocation — usually Jan 1 – Dec 31 of the leave year.
01/01/2025 – 31/12/2025
4
Required · Int
New Leaves Allocated Int
Number of leave days to grant. For annual leave enter 21 (or 30 for senior staff).
Ahmed 21 days Fatima 30 (sick) James 21 days
5
Optional · Check
Add unused leaves from previous period Check
Tick this to carry forward any unused balance from the prior allocation period.
⚠ KSA law limits carry-forward of annual leave. Do not carry forward more than what is permitted — consult HR policy.
After saving
✓
Submit
Click Submit to activate the allocation. The employee can now apply for leave of this type. Once submitted, use Amend to correct errors.

EOSB Calculation

HR Suite › Lifecycle · End-of-Service Benefit per KSA Art. 84–88
Lifecycle
Path: HR Suite → Lifecycle → EOSB Calculation → New
1
Required · Link
Employee
Joining date, last working day, and service duration auto-fill from the Employee master.
James Joined 01/03/2025 · LWD 31/05/2027 = 2.25 yrs
2
Required · Select
Separation Reason
Resignation / Employer Termination / Mutual Agreement / Death / Disability. Determines the applicable entitlement fraction.
James Resignation → ⅓ of daily wage × 365 × yrs (for <5 yrs service)
💡 Employer termination without cause = full EOSB. Resignation <5 yrs = ⅓; 5–10 yrs = ⅔; >10 yrs = full.
3
Required · Currency
Last Basic Salary
The monthly basic salary used as the base for EOSB. Do NOT include allowances.
James SAR 11,000
4
Read-only · Currency
EOSB Amount
Calculated automatically: (Last Basic ÷ 30) × 365 × Years × Fraction. Review the formula breakdown before submitting.
James (SAR 11,000÷30) × 365 × 2.25 × ⅓ = SAR 10,028
5
Optional · Currency
Loan Deduction
Outstanding employee loan balance. Deducted from final settlement amount.
James SAR 0 (loan fully repaid by May 2027)
