End of Service Benefit (EOSB)
Mandatory severance per Art. 84–88. Auto-created when Termination Notice is submitted. System calculates based on years of service, reason for exit, and deductions.
Must pay within 5 days of exit Auto-created from Termination Notice Art. 84–88
›
Service lengthRate (resignation)Rate (employer termination)
Under 2 yearsNo entitlementNo entitlement
2 – 5 years1/3 month per year1/2 month per year
5 – 10 years2/3 month per year1 month per year
Over 10 years1 month per year1 month per year
5-day rule: KSA Labor Law requires EOSB payment within 5 days of the last working day. The Final Settlement SLA alert sends daily escalating notifications to Finance from day 3 if payment has not been processed.
What the system adds together
EOSB base amount — calculated from service years × applicable rate
+ Annual Leave Disbursement (unused leave balance × daily wage)
+ Any other entitlements (unpaid salary, expenses)
− Loan balance outstanding
− Training bond repayment (if resigned during bond period)
− Any other deductions
Net Final Settlement = Total entitlements − Total deductionsFinance approves → Journal Entry → Bank transfer

Disciplinary Procedure
Full formal procedure for serious violations — suspension, written warning, or termination. Documents every required Saudi procedural step: investigation, hearing, notice, decision, appeal.
High-stakes — audit trail critical Follow every step before dismissal
›
Legal risk: Dismissal without a documented procedure is legally challenged as wrongful termination at the Ministry of HR Labor Courts. Every step below must be completed and documented.
Full disciplinary workflow
Investigation Record — form the investigation committee, document findings
Employee Warning Notice — employee must be formally notified and given 5 working days to respond in writing
Hold hearing — document proceedings in Disciplinary Procedure record
Disciplinary Decision Log — record outcome: Warning / Penalty / Suspension / Termination
Employee receives 15 days to file a Disciplinary Appeal if contesting the decision
If Termination: submit Termination Notice → EOSB auto-created (zero if Art. 80 dismissal for cause)
Art. 80 termination for cause: Employees dismissed for cause (e.g., fraud, assault, falsifying records) are not entitled to EOSB. The system sets EOSB to zero automatically when Termination Reason = Art. 80.

›

On any Salary Structure Assignment, two icons appear next to each salary component field:

Clock icon — History
Opens a history dialog showing all previous override values for this component, with a year filter and full table of changes (date, old value, new value, notes, who changed it).
Pen icon — Edit / Override

How overrides are applied
HR clicks the pen icon on a salary component → enters effective date, new value, and notes
If effective date is today or past: override applied immediately, status = Applied
If effective date is in the future: status = Pending — the salary component is unchanged until that date
Daily scheduler checks all Pending overrides — applies any whose effective date has arrived
Audit trail: Every override — including who made it, when, the old value, and the notes — is stored in the SalaryComponentOverride DocType and visible via the clock icon on the assignment. This satisfies Ministry of HR audit requirements for documented salary changes.
